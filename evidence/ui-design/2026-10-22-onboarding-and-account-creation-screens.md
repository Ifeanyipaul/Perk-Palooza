## What the screens show

### Screenshot 1 — Onboarding and welcome screen
The design shows onboarding content explaining that users can know when a card has been used, alongside a welcome screen with sign-in and sign-up actions, a business registration entry point, and a note that participating businesses are verified before onboarding.

### Screenshot 2 — Account-created confirmation
The design shows an account creation success screen with the message “Welcome To Perk Palooza” and confirmation that the account was successfully created. Part of an OTP verification screen is also visible beside it.

## Product relevance

These screens document the proposed first-time user journey:
- Introduce the product's purpose and card-use tracking.
- Allow an existing user to sign in or a new user to sign up.
- Provide a route for businesses to register.
- Communicate the business-verification principle.
- Confirm successful account creation after the relevant registration/verification flow.
