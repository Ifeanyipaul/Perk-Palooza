## Product/team lead

**Name:** Paul Ifeanyichukwu Gabriel  
**Role:** Team Lead coordinating requirements, tasks and progress

### Responsibilities

- Coordinate review of the PRD and keep the team aligned on product requirements.
- Break agreed requirements into trackable GitHub Issues.
- Clarify expected outcomes and acceptance criteria with relevant contributors.
- Assign work to the actual responsible team member and track progress.
- Coordinate product decisions and ensure unresolved questions have owners.
- Monitor blockers, dependencies and milestones.
- Keep product documentation and progress records current.
- Coordinate review of completed work against agreed acceptance criteria.
- Communicate scope changes and their impact to the team.

### Scope boundaries

This document records the team lead's coordination responsibilities. It does not imply that the team lead personally implemented, designed, tested, or approved work unless separate evidence shows that contribution.

## Ownership rules

- Each issue should have one directly responsible assignee where possible.
- Contributors should report blockers in the issue rather than only in private messages.
- Product decisions should be recorded in the decision log and linked from affected issues.
- Engineering implementation details remain with the responsible engineering contributors.
- Approvals must be recorded only by people who actually reviewed or authorised the work.
