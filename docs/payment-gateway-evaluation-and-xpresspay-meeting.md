# Payment Gateway Evaluation and XpressPay Meeting

**Project:** Perk Palooza  
**Date documented:** 25 October 2026  
**Owner / role:** Product leadership and coordination  
**Stakeholder:** Vanessa, CEO of Perk Palooza

## Discussion summary

I discussed payment gateway options with Vanessa, CEO of Perk Palooza, to evaluate how payments should be handled for the digital voucher platform.

I recommended evaluating the following providers:
- Paystack
- Kora
- Flutterwave

The discussion covered the payment flow and the need to assess transaction fees, settlement arrangements, payment splitting, settlement timelines, and merchant onboarding before selecting a provider.

## XpressPay follow-up

We received a response from Yusuf Arowosaye at XpressPay. He confirmed that XpressPay supports automatic split settlement, which may suit Perk Palooza's marketplace model, and proposed a virtual meeting to walk through the use case and answer the outstanding questions.

**Meeting scheduled:** Tuesday, 27 October 2026, at 12:00 PM (Nigeria time, WAT).

XpressPay will arrange the meeting invitation and share an overview deck ahead of the call.

## Items to clarify during the meeting

1. Whether customer payments can be split automatically between Perk Palooza and the relevant business.
2. Whether the business's share can be settled directly to its bank account, with Perk Palooza's transaction fee deducted.
3. The applicable transaction, split-settlement, and payout fees.
4. Settlement timelines, payout controls, refunds, and failed or disputed transactions.
5. Merchant onboarding requirements, compliance obligations, integration steps, and launch support.
