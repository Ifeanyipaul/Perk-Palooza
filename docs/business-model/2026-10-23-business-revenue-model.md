# Perk Palooza — Business Revenue Model Update

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Workstream:** Product strategy and monetisation  
**Status:** Proposed model for discussion and validation; not a record of final approval

## Summary

Perk Palooza can generate revenue from businesses as well as customers. The proposed approach is to help businesses attract customers through vouchers and campaigns, understand campaign performance, and pay for additional business tools or verified sales generated through the platform.

The revenue streams below are options to validate with the CEO, development team, and prospective businesses. The example prices and rates are illustrative, not approved pricing.

## 1. Paid featured vouchers and business promotion

Businesses could pay for additional visibility on the platform. Potential placements include:

- **Featured voucher:** A voucher is highlighted in a prominent placement for an agreed period.
- **Featured business:** A business is promoted in relevant categories or recommendation areas.
- **Sponsored campaign:** A business pays to promote a time-limited offer, such as a seasonal campaign.

**Illustrative example:** If 20 businesses each pay ₦5,000 for a featured campaign, gross revenue would be ₦100,000.

The product should make paid placements distinguishable from ordinary listings and define the placement duration, audience, and reporting provided.

## 2. Campaign analytics

Perk Palooza could offer businesses reporting that helps them understand campaign performance. Possible metrics include:

- Voucher views and clicks.
- Voucher saves or claims, where supported.
- Redemptions and verified completed sales.
- Conversion rates between stages, where the required tracking exists.
- Campaign comparisons and downloadable reports in a paid tier.

A free tier could include basic statistics, while advanced reports could be included in a paid subscription or sold as an add-on. Avoid charging separately for analytics that are already included in a subscription unless the distinction is clear.

**Illustrative example:** An advanced analytics package could be tested at ₦10,000 per month. This price needs validation with businesses.

Analytics should only report metrics the product can reliably collect; views, clicks, redemptions, and completed sales are different events and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 3. Premium business subscriptions

A recurring subscription could give businesses access to more tools and campaign capacity.

| Proposed tier | Possible inclusions | Illustrative price |
|---|---|---:|
| Free | Business profile, basic voucher creation, basic statistics | ₦0 |
| Growth | More campaign capacity, standard analytics, optional discounted promotion | ₦10,000/month |
| Premium | Advanced analytics, multiple active campaigns, enhanced reporting and visibility tools | ₦25,000/month |

These tiers and prices are hypotheses for validation, not approved product decisions. Before implementation, define feature limits, billing periods, cancellation, renewal, and what happens to campaigns when a subscription expires.

**Illustrative calculation:** 100 businesses on a ₦10,000 monthly plan would produce ₦1,000,000 in gross monthly subscription revenue before expenses, taxes, refunds, and payment-processing costs.

## 4. Commission on verified sales

Perk Palooza could earn an agreed commission when the platform demonstrably generates an eligible sale for a participating business.

**Illustrative example:**

- Eligible completed sale: ₦20,000
- Agreed commission rate: 5%
- Perk Palooza commission: ₦1,000
- Business balance before its other costs: ₦19,000

This example assumes the full sale value is commissionable and excludes any separate fees, discounts, refunds, or taxes. The business must agree to the commission terms before the campaign starts.

Potential verification methods include unique voucher codes, tracked checkout links, business-confirmed redemptions, or an integrated payment/POS flow. The product and operations process should define how to handle cancelled orders, refunds, duplicate redemptions, partial payments, and disputed sales.

Commission should be calculated only on the agreed eligible amount and only after the defined verification/completion condition is met. Do not treat a voucher view or claim as a completed sale.

## Illustrative combined revenue scenario

The following scenario shows how multiple revenue streams could coexist. It is an example, not a forecast.

| Revenue source | Assumption | Gross monthly revenue |
|---|---|---:|
| Featured campaigns | 20 businesses × ₦5,000 | ₦100,000 |
| Growth subscriptions | 30 businesses × ₦10,000 | ₦300,000 |
| Sales commission | ₦5,000,000 eligible sales × 5% | ₦250,000 |
| **Total** | Before costs and taxes | **₦650,000** |

These assumptions must be validated. The model should avoid double-counting the same fee or revenue event and should account for payment costs, discounts, refunds, taxes, and operational expenses.

## Suggested validation and implementation sequence

1. Interview prospective businesses to learn which value they would pay for: visibility, analytics, subscription tools, or sales-based fees.
2. Confirm with the CEO which models to test first and document any approved rates separately from proposals.
3. Start with the simplest measurable options: business onboarding, featured vouchers, and an optional commission agreement for verified sales.
4. Define the event and data model needed to track impressions/views, clicks, claims, redemptions, and completed sales separately.
5. Design business reporting and billing so businesses can see what they are paying for and how campaign results are measured.
6. Introduce premium tiers and advanced analytics after usage data and customer feedback demonstrate demand.

## Product decisions still to confirm

- Which revenue streams will be part of the first release?
- Will featured placements be charged per campaign, per day, or through a subscription?
- What features and limits belong in each business plan?
- What constitutes a verified, commissionable sale, and how will it be confirmed?
- Will commission be calculated on the gross order value or another agreed base?
- How will refunds, cancellations, discounts, taxes, and payment fees be handled?
- What business-facing analytics can the current product reliably collect?
- What pricing and terms have been explicitly approved by the CEO?

## Product leadership note

This update documents the proposed monetisation model and the questions requiring validation. The prices, 5% commission example, and revenue scenarios are illustrative; they should not be represented as final company decisions until confirmed.
