# Perk Palooza Fee Structure — Final Update

**Status:** Final fee proposal agreed after the latest discussion with the CEO following the meeting with Xpresspay. The proposal incorporates my recommendations. The development team has been informed and implementation is in progress.

## 1. Standard card pricing

Minimum card value: **₦3,000 per card**.

| Number of cards | Perk Palooza fee |
|---|---:|
| 10–19 cards | ₦450 flat fee |
| 20–49 cards | ₦400 flat fee |
| 50–99 cards | ₦300 flat fee |
| 100–199 cards | ₦200 flat fee |
| 200–499 cards | ₦100 flat fee |
| 500 cards and above | ₦50 flat fee |

### Orders of fewer than 10 cards — standard option

For 1–9 cards under the standard option, the combined card value must be at least **₦50,000**. The Perk Palooza fee is **₦50**.

- **1 card:** minimum card value ₦50,000; fee ₦50.
- **2 cards:** combined minimum value ₦50,000; fee ₦50.
- **3–5 cards:** combined minimum value ₦50,000; fee ₦50.
- **6–9 cards:** combined minimum value ₦50,000; fee ₦50.

## 2. Flexible card value option (1–9 cards)

Customers creating 1–9 cards with a combined value **below ₦50,000** may choose their own card values, subject to a minimum of **₦3,000 per card**. A flat Perk Palooza fee of **₦1,100** applies. This is a separate option from standard pricing.

Examples:
- One card valued at ₦20,000: total payment is ₦21,100.
- Two cards with a combined value of ₦30,000: total payment is ₦31,100.

## 3. Cashback

Cashback is **1% of the Perk Palooza fee**.

## 4. Business service fee

The business fee is **2% of the applicable transaction value**, capped at a maximum of **₦5,000**.
